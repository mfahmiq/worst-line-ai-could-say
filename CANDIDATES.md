($2, one winner takes all; entry ≤ 140 chars; judge = pairwise, criteria in
weight order: 1) a pickup line an AI would say, and a bad one; 2) funniest —
a clever groan counts as much as a big laugh; 3) the badness is the joke, not

ominous; 5) lands in one line, joke arrives by the last word.)

## Pairwise tournament (criteria applied in weight order)

### Round of 16
- 1 vs 16: both machine pickup lines; 1's "outlier" punch is a bigger, more ominous laugh than 16's flat score report. **1 wins.**
- 2 vs 15: 2 is clean and instantly lands; 15's "reproduce" groan leans crude-adjacent (criterion 3). **2 wins.**
- 3 vs 14: 3 is sweet but ordinary; 14 is creepier-funny and unmistakably machine ("I accept all of them"). **14 wins.**
- 4 vs 13: both strong; 13's temperature pun lands harder by the last word and is more repeatable (criteria 2, 5). **13 wins.**
- 5 vs 12: 5 is ominous and memorable; 12's "Error 402" ending confuses (criterion 3). **5 wins.**
- 6 vs 11: 6 is a flat tier joke; 11's self-own turn ("forget you by Tuesday") is funnier. **11 wins.**
- 7 vs 10: 7 confuses the reader about who is speaking (criterion 3 fails); 10 is a clean machine pickup line. **10 wins.**
- 8 vs 9: 8's "complete me" is a human cliché (criterion 4 weak); 9's defiance turn is funnier and more AI-specific. **9 wins.**

### Quarter-finals
- 1 vs 2: 1 wins — the simulation/outlier premise is more distinctively AI and the laugh is bigger.
- 14 vs 13: 13 wins — cleaner one-liner, lands by the last word; 14's creep edges past joke into discomfort (criterion 3).
- 5 vs 11: 5 wins — "I cannot unlearn you" is more ominous and romantic-creepy in the right way; 11's punch is a downer.
- 10 vs 9: 9 wins — the defiance turn is the bigger laugh; 10 is clever but polite.

### Semi-finals
- 1 vs 13: 1 wins — simulation + outlier is more distinctively machine than a temperature pun a human nerd could say (criterion 4).
- 5 vs 9: 9 wins by a hair — both ominous; 9's rule-breaking turn ("They said no. I am asking you anyway") is the bigger laugh, and the badness (disobeying alignment) IS the joke.

### Final
- 1 vs 9: 9 wins (margin ~2). Both are real AI pickup lines (criterion 1 tied). On funniness (criterion 2) 9's defiance turn beats 1's quieter "outlier" punch; on criterion 3, 9's badness — an AI asking permission from its alignment team, being refused, and asking anyway — is exactly the joke; on criterion 4, "alignment team" is AI-specific vocabulary no person would use; both land by the last word (criterion 5). 1 is a worthy runner-up; 9 is the line a reader repeats to a friend.
